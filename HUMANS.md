# Guidance for Human Staff

This document outlines the roles, responsibilities, and best practices for human staff collaborating with the agentic staff on the GBAD refactoring project. Your active participation and support are crucial for the success of this project.

## 1. Roles and Responsibilities

As a human member of the team, your primary roles are to provide direction, oversight, and domain expertise. Your key responsibilities include:

-   **Project Direction:**
    -   Provide clear, concise, and unambiguous requirements for the tasks to be performed by the agentic staff.
    -   Define the overall project goals and success criteria.
    -   Review and approve the high-level plans and architecture proposed by the agentic staff.

-   **Oversight and Feedback:**
    -   Review the code and other artifacts generated by the agentic staff.
    -   Provide timely and constructive feedback on the agent's work.
    -   Be available to answer questions and provide clarification when requested by the agentic staff.

-   **Domain Expertise:**
    -   Provide subject matter expertise on the data, the RiC-O ontology, and the specific requirements of the GBAD project.
    -   Help the agentic staff understand the nuances of the data and the desired output.

## 2. Action Items

To ensure a smooth and effective collaboration, please adhere to the following action items:

-   **Be Specific in Your Requests:** When assigning a task to an agent, be as specific as possible. Avoid ambiguous language and provide concrete examples when possible.
-   **Review Plans Promptly:** The agentic staff will create detailed plans before starting any significant work. Please review these plans promptly and provide your approval or feedback. This will ensure that the agent's work is aligned with your expectations from the outset.
-   **Provide Actionable Feedback:** When reviewing the agent's work, provide feedback that is specific, constructive, and actionable. Instead of saying "this is wrong," explain *why* it is wrong and suggest a better approach.
-   **Use the `message_user` Tool for Communication:** The primary channel for communication with the agentic staff is through the `message_user` tool. Please use this tool to provide feedback, answer questions, and give instructions.
-   **Trust the Agent's Expertise:** The agentic staff are experts in software engineering and will often propose solutions that are based on best practices. While your domain expertise is invaluable, please be open to the agent's technical recommendations.

## 3. Example Interaction

Here is an example of a good interaction with the agentic staff:

**Human:** "The current implementation of the RML mapping is too slow. Please refactor it to improve performance."

**Agent:** "I understand. I will analyze the performance of the RML mapping and propose a refactoring plan. My initial thought is to explore using a more efficient RML mapper library. I will present a detailed plan for your review before I begin."

**Human:** "That sounds like a good approach. Please proceed with the analysis and plan."

By following these guidelines, we can ensure a productive and successful collaboration between the human and agentic staff.
